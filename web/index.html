      How to read this

      Relative strength, not price

      Each theme is an equal-weight basket. The bar is its return minus the benchmark over the window. Positive = outrunning the market; negative = dragging.

      Rank is the signal

      Themes are ranked against each other. Top 15% = leader (long), bottom 15% = laggard (short). This cross-sectional momentum is the one cut the backtest validated.

      What's proven, what isn't

      Net of costs the long/short ran +29.6%/yr · Sharpe 1.23 over ~6y — on the AI subset. The full universe shown here is not yet re-validated. Treat it as a map, not a trade.
