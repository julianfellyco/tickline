Is it trending — and does the crowd agree?

One row per market theme. Two simple questions: is the price in an uptrend (above its 200-day line and beating the S&P), and is the crowd talking about it? The colour tells you the rest.

—

🔵EarlyTrending up, crowd still quiet. The good kind.
🟢ConfirmedTrending up and everyone's piling in.
🟡Hype trapLoud buzz but the price is falling. Careful.
🔴DeadFalling and forgotten. Avoid.
⚪SidewaysNo clear trend yet. Wait.

Themes

This is not financial advice. It's a plain snapshot of price trend and news buzz — a starting point for your own research, not a buy/sell signal.
Trend = is the price above its 200-day average and beating the S&P over 3 months.
Loud vs Quiet = how many news stories the theme got this week versus the typical theme. More than average → Loud (the crowd's attention is on it); fewer → Quiet (under the radar). It measures attention only — not whether the news is good or bad. That's why a falling theme can still be "Loud". Data is delayed and can be wrong.
