01 / layers / quant + algo + ai

Three layers. One framework.

Most retail trading projects pick one — rules, statistics, or ML — and pretend the other two don't matter. tickline composes all three, each in the role where it's actually safe to use.

L1 · QUANT SHIPPED

Statistical rigor

the foundation

Cost-aware backtest engine with no lookahead. Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown, Calmar, profit factor. Walk-forward validation that exposes overfit before live capital does.

  • 15bps round-trip baked in
  • Anchored + rolling walk-forward
  • Triple-barrier accounting
L2 · ALGO SHIPPED

Rule-based signals

the interpretable core

Baseline strategies that say exactly what they will do, when, and why. Easy to read, easy to debug, easy to compare against fancier ideas.

  • SMA crossover (20 / 50)
  • RSI mean-reversion (14, 30/55)
  • Strategy ABC for adding more
L3 · AI SHIPPED

Meta-labeler gate

the filter, not the prophet

Gradient boosting trained on 13 engineered features filters which algo signals to trust. AI as a gate over interpretable rules — never as a black-box price predictor.

  • HistGradientBoostingClassifier
  • 13 features · permutation imp.
  • AUC + accuracy out-of-sample

04 / discipline / non-negotiable

Four hard rules. Non-negotiable.

RULE 01

No live capital until paper trading shows ≥3 months of consistency.

Backtests lie. Paper trading lies less. Real money is the only honest oracle, and we don't ask the oracle until we've earned the right.

RULE 02

Every backtest includes fees and slippage.

Defaults are intentionally pessimistic. If a strategy survives this engine, it has a chance in production. If it dies here, it dies for free.

RULE 03

No overfitting.

If a strategy needs eight hyperparameters to look good in-sample, it's noise. Walk-forward exposes that. Either it survives forward windows, or it doesn't ship.

RULE 04

Position sizing beats strategy selection.

A great signal with bad sizing still blows up. Vol-targeting and fractional Kelly come before finding the next clever idea. Survival first, then alpha.
